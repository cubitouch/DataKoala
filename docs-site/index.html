FROM QUESTION TO ANSWER

Follow the question,
not the tool.

Build an analytical query visually, take over in SQL when it gets complex, and turn the result into something you can understand at a glance.

01

BUILD

Begin with one question.

Pick a table and a metric in the visual Builder. Start with a single series before the analysis gets more complex.

02

VISUALIZE

See the pattern.

Turn your result into an area chart and spot the trends worth investigating, without leaving the query.

03

EJECT TO SQL

Go beyond the Builder.

Switch to editable SQL for richer expressions, joins and custom analysis, then explore the answer as a visualization.

GRAFANA DATA SOURCES · POWERED BY GCX

Observe systems through data.

Explore metrics, logs, and traces from Grafana-backed data sources in one focused desktop workspace.

01

PROMETHEUS

Follow a metric trend.

Explore time-series behavior with PromQL and discover changes worth investigating.

02

LOKI

Find the volume spike.

Break down log volume over time, then narrow your investigation to the interesting interval.

03

TEMPO

Inspect the trace.

Follow the spans behind a slow request and inspect its waterfall to see where time was spent.

EXPLORE FURTHER

Stay close to the details.

CONNECTIONS

Connect to the data you already have.

Create and manage connections to PostgreSQL, BigQuery, SQLite, local files and Grafana-backed observability sources.

VISUALIZATIONS

Find the right shape for the answer.

Switch between line, bar, area, scatter, treemap and sunburst charts. Explore hierarchical results without losing the original query.

STRUCTURED DATA

Look inside complex values.

Inspect structured data and nested fields in context, including JSON values and parsed log metadata, rather than flattening everything first.

BUILT FOR THE INVESTIGATOR

Your tools should help you think,
not hide what they did.

01

Local-first

Your data remains on your machine and source services. DataKoala connects to the systems you already use instead of becoming another place to copy data into.

02

Transparent

Visual builders produce queries you can inspect and reuse. Drop into raw query mode whenever you need more control.

03

Open source

DataKoala is licensed AGPL-3.0-or-later. Inspect the implementation, follow the roadmap, and report the rough edges in public.
